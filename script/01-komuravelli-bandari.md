# Episode 1 — కొమురవెల్లి మల్లన్న · "పసుపు ఎక్కడిది?"

**Two parts** · Telugu narration, English subtitles · 1080×1920 · 2D illustration + parallax

> Replaces the two earlier scripts. This one is a story, not a catalogue.

## The retell sentence

> *"The turmeric people throw at Mallanna's temple? He stole it. Disguised as a gardener,
> for months. And a goddess lost her hand getting him home."*

## Why two parts, and where the cut is

Told whole at a storyteller's pace, this runs about two and a half minutes. Karunakar said
not to compress, so it's two episodes. The cut falls on the strongest beat in the story —
**the moment the con breaks: he has the bandari, and someone has seen.**

Each part keeps the series signature (object → story → object) on its own terms:
- **Part 1** opens on turmeric in *the viewer's* hand and ends on turmeric in *Mallanna's*
  hand, for the first time. Your hand / his hand.
- **Part 2** opens on his hand and ends on yours. The loop closes across both.

## Decisions baked in

- **Destination unnamed** (Karunakar, 2026-11-22). He goes "far away." The ones who chase
  him are "the guards." Every dramatic beat survives; nothing is invented or contradicted.
- **One card per part**, at the hinge. Part 1: the condition. Part 2: the payoff.
- **"Why here?"** answered in Part 2: *he came back to this very hill.*
- Sthala puranam frame once, at the top of Part 1, then full confidence.

## Notes for the Telugu read

Written as spoken storytelling, not prose. Short sentences. The pauses are in the dashes.

**One choice for Karunakar to make on the whole channel:** this is standard spoken Telugu.
A **Telangana** read — *వచ్చిండు, చేసిండు, ఆగిండు, పెట్టిండ్రు* — would be truer to this god
and this audience, and would separate the channel from every Andhra-accented mythology
reel. It is a ten-minute pass, and he should do it himself: he'll get the ear right and I
would get it almost right, which is worse.

Lines I'm least sure carry in Telugu:
- P1 §2 **"రాయి కాదు — పుట్టమట్టి."** Two words. I want it to land like a correction.
- P1 §6 **"అందరూ నమ్మేదాకా — ఆగాడు."** The whole con in one verb. Is *ఆగాడు* strong enough,
  or does it want *ఓపిక పట్టాడు*?
- P2 §6 **"మీరు చల్లే ప్రతి పిడికెడు — గంగమ్మ చేయి. మల్లన్న ఓపిక."** This is the
  storyteller's closing line, not a sourced claim — a narrator's reflection inside the
  sthala-puranam frame. If it feels like too much, cut it and end on *ప్రతి ఆదివారం*.
