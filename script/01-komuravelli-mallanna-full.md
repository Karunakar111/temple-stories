## Which telling this is

**This is the Oggu Katha marriage story — the *bandari* episode — as the Oggu Pujaris of
the Kuruma and Golla communities sing it at Komuravelli.** Research §2a, §21.

It is **one telling, not the telling.** Different Oggu troupes tell it differently, and
that is normal in the tradition, not an error in it. The narration says so out loud, in
its second breath: *"ఇక్కడి ఒగ్గు పూజారులు పాడే కథ ఇది."* Every claim about the story is
framed by that line.

The Mallanna story has **no written scripture** (§2 [A]) — the god's cousin Khandoba in
Maharashtra has a Sanskrit text, this one has only the song. Folklorists have since
transcribed Oggu Katha, so the episode says *"దీనికి రాతప్రతి లేదు"* — *it has no
scripture* — and **not** "this story is in no book", which was the false claim removed in
v1.1.

### What is deliberately left out, and why

| Left out | Status | Why |
|---|---|---|
| **The destination, and the two pursuers by name** | The tradition names them | **Karunakar's decision, 2026-11-22.** Told cold to a new audience it reads as communal content regardless of intent. Every dramatic beat survives without it. This is an omission, openly made — not a claim that the tradition is silent. |
| **Komaranna founding the temple** | [?] untraceable | Cannot be traced to the temple or Endowments. §2b. |
| **Bapana Ratnangi, the third wife, and the Oggu priests' descent from her** | [C] one source | The best single find in four passes, and it explains the priesthood — but one source. Hold for its own episode. §18. |
| **Born of Shiva's second sweat drop** | [B] | A different kanda. Would bloat the setup. §11. |
| **"About five hundred years old"** | [B] | Stated as *they say* only, or not at all. §1, §16. |
| **Specific jatara dates** | [?] varies by year and source | Only "Sankranti to Ugadi, Sundays". §13. |

### What the episode says it is unsure about

Said on screen, not hidden in a doc: the story is sung rather than written, this is the
telling heard here, and other villages tell it other ways — which is also the comment ask.
