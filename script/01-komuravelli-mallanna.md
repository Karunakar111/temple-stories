## Writing notes

- **Shot 1 is the whole episode.** If it doesn't land in three seconds, nothing after it
  matters. It opens on an absence — no book — not on a greeting or a logo.
- **The last line answers the first.** "No one wrote his story down" → "it lives only in
  the singing." Close the loop and stop. No call to action over the final image.
- Shot 4 is written to be cut **on the drum**. Lock the music before animating it.
- Shot 6 must be **visually symmetrical**. The script says the two communities are equal;
  if the composition doesn't, the composition wins and the script is a lie.
- Shot 8 drops to the dolu alone. Let the last two seconds be almost silent.

## Iconography — get these right or cut the shot

- **Mallanna**: horse, dog, sword, turmeric. These identify him. A generic Shiva will read
  as wrong to anyone who knows this god.
- **The Oggu performer** (research §3): wide pant tied at the knee, coloured shirt,
  coloured head cloth, *gavvala darshanam* (seven cowrie shells), *ambarala golusu*
  (garland bearing Mallanna's portrait), and a **dolu**. Not a generic folk musician.
- **Patnam** is a floor drawing in turmeric and coloured powder, made on stone — not a
  decorative festival rangoli.
- **No AI-generated Telugu lettering inside any image.** All text is real text in Remotion.

## Word count

**95 Telugu words over 82 s ≈ 1.16 words/s.** That is on the sparse side even for Telugu,
which is the intent — this script is built to leave room for images and for the drum. But
it is an estimate, and estimates of narration length are usually wrong.

**The audio is the clock.** Generate the narration, measure it, then set
`durationInFrames` and every shot boundary from the real waveform — not from this table.
If the real read comes in under ~70 s, the fix is to hold shots longer, not to add words.

## Open, before this is final

Resolve from research §6 — none of these block the script as written, but shot 2 and
shot 8 get better with real detail:

1. Has Karunakar been to Komuravelli? One first-hand image beats ten generated ones.
2. Does his family know a different telling of the marriage? Credit it if so.
3. Telugu register: this is written in plain spoken Telugu. Should it carry more
   **Telangana** flavour? He should rewrite any line that sounds like a newsreader.

## ⚠ The Telugu needs a native read before anything is generated

I wrote the Telugu above and I am reasonably confident in it, but I am not a native
speaker and this is a channel for people who are. **Karunakar should read every line out
loud and rewrite anything that sounds written rather than spoken.** Specifically worth
his ear:

- Shot 7, `ఇద్దరు పూజారులు` — I mean two *priesthoods*, two traditions. Does this carry it?
- Throughout — the register is plain spoken Telugu. For a Telangana subject, a Telangana
  cadence would be better, and only he can put it there.

A line that sounds slightly off in Telugu will undo all the research accuracy above.
